STATUS DEFINITIONS
------------------
CONTEXT: course framing, conceptual explanation, recap or publication data.
  All applicable functional statements are mapped to adjacent requirement
  groups; context does not count as working functionality.
VERIFIED_SUBSET: a specifically bounded operation has engine regressions and
  live UI evidence. Other operations on the same pages can still be missing.
PARTIAL: related executable functionality exists, but the specified procedure
  cannot yet be performed exactly from beginning to end.
MISSING: the required simulated workflow/model is absent.
BOUNDARY: real hardware, electrical installation, Windows administration,
  vendor protocols/tools or licenses cannot be validated by this offline app.
  Any potential training model is separately described; NOT a parity pass.
