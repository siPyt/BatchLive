PASS 12: SFC ACTION LIFETIMES GROUNDED IN THE ORIGINAL TIMING CHART
----------------------------------------------------------------
Original p276 image examined locally, not inferred solely from extracted text.
Independent action runtime tracks active/pending/fired status and stored timers.
N/D/L stop executing at step exit; P can fire once after time or a structured
condition; S persists across steps until reset/termination; SD remains pending
after exit; DS cancels if the step exits before its delay; SL expires across
steps. R identifies a stored action and stops it on entry without inverse writes.
The p276 timing matrix, step-exit before/at/after delay, delayed/reset SD,
stored expiry, pulse once, expression-subset timing and termination are tested.
Fractional100ms scans do not acquire an extra scan through accumulated roundoff.
Incoming step actions/reset execute at the transition boundary. Held routines
pause simulated timers; resume retains them; reset clears memory; RUN from
COMPLETE starts clean. Batch phases share the same engine and clock scaling.
